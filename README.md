# CutAngle

**Linear cutting optimiser for tubes and bars, with mitred and angled cuts.**

Enter the parts you need, the stock you have and the lengths your supplier sells. CutAngle plans the cuts, uses your stock and offcuts first, and tells you what to buy. It also gives the weight and the painted area of the useful parts, and a quote with profit margin, exported to Excel.

## Download
Go to **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download `CutAngleV1.2.0.exe`. It is portable: run it directly, nothing to install.

**Windows may show a warning** ("Windows protected your PC") because the program is not digitally signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Features
- Tubes, bars, T bars and profiles, with a face for each part
- Mitred and angled cuts, with an angle for each end
- Stock, supplier lengths and reusable offcuts; the program spends stock first
- Priority parts, optimised first so the job can start cutting
- Saw width and trimming of bar ends
- Weight and painted area (list of ready-made materials, plus your own)
- Quote with prices up to 4 decimals and profit margin; Excel export and printing
- Save and open jobs as XML
- Languages: English, Português, Español, Français, Deutsch
